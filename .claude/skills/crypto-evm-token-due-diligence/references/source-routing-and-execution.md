# Source routing and parallel execution

Use in broad diligence; focused work selects only sources needed for its question.
The objective is evidence for each material risk surface, not a visit to every website.
Source features and chain support change: follow current official documentation and
exact-token links. A ticker, trending rank or search snippet never resolves identity.

## Choose sources by the fact they can establish

Start with exact-address pool discovery and target-linked official project channels in
parallel with provider preparation. Use those channels for a bounded public team/role,
delivery and operating-history pass before optional repository/social depth; commits
alone are not the credibility assessment. Assign sources by the missing fact so a long
source checklist does not consume the budget without changing the decision. After identity/pins are resolved, deepen only the
sources that supply a missing fact or material counterevidence. Prefer background reads
with `WebFetch`/`WebSearch` and documented APIs. JavaScript-only content that remains
unreadable is a recorded gap or alternate-source task. No personal Chrome tabs, authentication bypass, trading or
messages are needed for diligence. User-requested sources receive a recorded attempt or
a concrete access/applicability explanation.

The source table above is the menu, not a checklist: within the standard scope a lane
visits a source only when its brief's checklist or a conclusion-changing trigger names the
fact it can establish. In broad diligence, include Pump.fun in the social trading screen and Pons/Long in
launch-origin discovery only under that rule. Reuse target-linked pages and creation evidence first; when
unresolved, make a bounded exact-address lookup on each applicable surface (or a scoped
search if no documented direct lookup exists). Known network incompatibility can close
applicability with a recorded reason; do not search namesake coins. Deepen launchpad
docs, announcements and source only when the target or a material prior-launch lead is
implicated. Focused work keeps its question's scope. These checks share the existing
deadline and alternate-source limit; they do not add time or a separate request budget.

For Robinhood Chain mainnet (4663) targets, use RH Trenches when it can add a missing
trader-activity fact or material promotion/creator lead. Bound the initial lookup to
30 seconds or the remaining collection window, whichever is shorter; deepen only on
a conclusion-changing lead within the original budget. Its absence or selective
coverage never blocks the other sources. On other chains, record not applicable for
this adapter without substituting a Robinhood namesake. The liquidity/market lane owns
the lookup and shares captured rows with the project/creator lane; do not fetch the
same tape in both lanes or treat RH Trenches and Fomo as two independent witnesses.

For Robinhood Chain mainnet (4663), select RH Scan or Robinscan for missing contract,
holder or history evidence within the existing explorer work. Neither is a mandatory
extra visit when the fact is already established. The coordinator owns source/proxy leads,
the liquidity lane owns holder/pool leads, and the project lane owns creation and
prior-launch leads. Reuse
exact transaction references from RH Trenches and share captured records across lanes;
the same underlying transaction is not a second independent trade. Initial access must
fit within 30 seconds or the remaining collection window, whichever is shorter; deepen
only for a material fact within the original budget. A switch to the other explorer or
the current official-documentation explorer uses the same one-alternate limit for the
blocked fact; do not chain a third source after that allowance is spent. Explorer reads
do not change the coordinator's dRPC-first RPC policy or create a separate request budget.

Robinscan's Fomo leaderboard is contextual: PnL, trade counts and volume are account-wide
across chains; only Top Holdings is filtered to Robinhood Chain. Repeated Fomo-derived
observations across Fomo, RH Trenches and Robinscan are not independent corroboration.
Keep these metrics separate from Robinscan's own capped wallet-PnL Beta estimates and
from verified target-token flows. Use exact-token leads within the assigned work;
no named-trader watchlist or ongoing wallet monitoring is implied.

Record source URL/query, exact chain/address, retrieval time, source observation/block
time if available, range/pages, evidence IDs and outcome: checked, unavailable,
unsupported, or not needed with reason. Retain conflicting values with their timestamps;
resolve units, pools, state and indexed lag before choosing a value. Several dashboards
may share upstream data: agreement is not automatically independent corroboration.
Reuse raw evidence in the bundle's discovery/context ledger; no new report schema is
required. Unknown or unsearched surfaces never become clean checks.

For market integrity, compare observed volume/trades with depth, unique makers and
sampled execution; examine repeated counterparties and promotion incentives when material.
Paid boosts, wash-like patterns or high turnover are leads, not proof of fraud or common
control. Keep organic-demand confidence separate from token powers and custody.

## One coordinator and bounded lanes

For broad work, delegate two independent `Agent` lanes when that tool is available and
active instructions permit them: **liquidity and market** and **project and creator**. Controls
stay with the coordinator, which owns RPC. Each lane receives a one-line pointer to the
self-contained brief that `broad_collect.py start` wrote to `lanes/<lane>/brief.md` (never
a pasted brief); it does not read this skill, its references or other runs, does not run
RPC or `investigation.py`, writes no scripts, spawns no agents, validates its note with
`compose --check`, and returns **one** note in the compose schema by the 4-minute cutoff.
`start` pre-charges each lane's web budget once; the coordinator reconciles the notes with
`bundle_assemble.py compose --lane …`. Do not spawn nested workers or split a short focused
question merely to use concurrency.

| Owner | Work and handoff |
| --- | --- |
| Coordinator | Exact identity/provider selection; the standard pipeline (runtime, metadata, controls, pools, balances, quotes, positions, receipts, actors, source match); token/proxy/role powers and surrounding contracts; presets for conclusion-changing reads; one RPC queue/cache, finite budget, deadline and report reconciliation. |
| Liquidity and market lane | Pool discovery, v2/v3/v4 custody and LP authority, holder concentration/unlocks, ordinary-holder execution and size quotes; own Fomo/Pump.fun social trading and applicable RH Trenches wallet-activity reads, reconciling dashboard/feed claims with supplied chain evidence. Share creator/profile leads with the project lane. |
| Project and creator lane | Official website/X, linked GitHub/audits, Pons/Long launch pages and announcements, real work versus claims, exact launch signer/recipient history, executed sales/proceeds and prior launches. Share launch/version/fee/custody evidence with the coordinator and the liquidity lane. Escalate cross-chain/commingled flows only if conclusion-changing. |

The liquidity/market lane owns valuation, venue/depth and social-participation context;
the project/creator lane owns product adoption, operating history and token-benefit claims.
Use [adoption-and-assessment.md](adoption-and-assessment.md), share underlying captures,
and fit the screen within the existing lanes, source alternatives and deadline. A market
leader earns evidence-based context, never an exemption from technical checks. Record
missing market history as Unverified rather than inferring poor adoption.

Each assignment receives the same chain/address, pin packet version, question/scope,
materiality/exit sizes, operational allowance, finite ceilings and their provenance,
handoff milestone and any imposed hard deadline; known evidence and its
storage location; owned surfaces and explicit stopping conditions. Return incremental handoff JSON in the supported draft shape, not a separate narrative
that the coordinator must reconstruct at the end. Return compact evidence rows with raw artifacts/query provenance, atomic amounts, proposed findings,
counterevidence and unknowns. Do not write separate final reports or claim unsupported
coverage. Use lane-specific artifact names/IDs; only the coordinator edits shared
manifest/report files. Distinguish observed exposure, ordinary policy terms and missing
coverage; workers cannot create new user requirements. Each material concern supplies
its mechanism and holder consequence. Return the next decision-changing check, not a
generic caution. The coordinator owns the final [decision review](decision-review.md).

Assign repository and X-post fetching to the project lane and share its pinned source
snapshot with controls analysis; assign each explorer/API query to one owner. Reuse
identical platform pages, posts and receipts across lanes, retaining one evidence ID
per captured artifact; a platform repost is not independent corroboration. When little
collection time remains, combine lanes or use fewer workers so setup does not consume
the window.

Workers request missing RPC facts through the coordinator; they do not inherit separate
paid budgets or reload credentials. Deduplicate by chain, queried address, pin hash,
method and canonical params, not code hash alone. Runtime analysis may be reused, while
storage, authority and balances remain per instance. Independent web reads may run in
parallel; honor provider rate limits and stop rules. If separate collectors are necessary,
use separate outputs and one shared `--session`, with coordinator-owned cache access;
per-command caps only narrow its aggregate limit. Include discovery, failures, external
tool charges and rechecks. All outputs stay below the session directory so operational
feedback shares one bounded file.

Use the [broad completion schedule](completion-and-delivery.md): target 5–7 minutes
including the report, lanes back by minute 4. Worker cutoffs are handoff milestones, not
final-delivery authority. Honor explicit hard deadlines and finite ceilings. Review the remaining
eleven-surface work plan before each batch and replan inadequate operational allowances
inside the same session's ceilings. Set collector timeout below that
remaining collection window and reserve requests for pin rechecks. No full holder replay,
large block-by-block log scan, repository build or deep trace by default: use snapshots,
bounded indexer discovery and narrowly selected receipts first. Deepen when a discrepancy
could change the conclusion and enough budget remains. Permit at most one alternate
source for a blocked fact under the shared runtime policy; avoid repeated failed reads.

Use [stopping and escalation](stopping-and-escalation.md) from intake onward. Queue
decision-critical unknowns ahead of optional context; when a useful authorized next
check fits, perform it. Each lane returns actual attempts, decision impact, the best
next route and its stopping basis with incomplete coverage rows. A binding user/provider
limit remains binding; an analyst estimate requires replanning and cannot establish
exhausted research. No worker cutoff independently authorizes final delivery.

At a handoff checkpoint reconcile lane identities, state/time, units, source matches,
contradictions and duplicated claims. Keep unsupported dimensions unknown and continue feasible consequential checks.
Only completed scoped work can pass the final completion gate; a genuine interruption
can be saved with `--checkpoint` internally. Freeze completed evidence, validate/render,
execute `bundle_assemble.py deliver`, and write from the accepted source without
refreshing sources for formatting. Record elapsed time, RPC attempts/cache hits and
coverage gaps and reviewed stopping reasons before claiming any speed or accuracy improvement; parallel work alone
is not a measured end-to-end benchmark.

## Structured capture and handoff

Use the [supported flow](supported-research-flow.md) for intake and lane JSON. Maintain
separate per-surface outcomes and next checks, including not-attempted checks. Queue
requests by decision impact before optional source expansion. API, web fetch, browser
view and export are separate capabilities: retain operation/access-mode/status/timing,
and distinguish raw downloads, browser snapshots and manual transcriptions in capture
provenance. Charge bounded external operations to the coordinator's shared session.
One failed API request never proves a whole service, source corpus or token is unavailable.
Use `operations.py capture` under that investigation root for browser-only or early
identity failures; no usable collection is required. Keep raw text outside reusable notes.

## Avoid serial expansion

Batch the first material authority/source requests with position and quote requests as
soon as addresses are known. Test source correspondence and history access before spending
on optional social pages. A source matrix records relevant attempts; it does not require
visiting every listed service. Deliver a lane update when a useful claim closes, including
exact registered IDs, so review and assembly overlap remaining collection. Deepen a completed
question only for a new contradiction or mechanism that could change the assessment.
