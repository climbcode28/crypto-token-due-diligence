# Narrow operational attribution

**Trigger:** authenticating a project's control/disclosure or an observed role is necessary to answer the user. Do not conduct broad personal-identity searches by default.

**Minimum evidence:** exact role/address/chain and time basis; authenticated project-control statement, existing signed public message, governance/deployment proof or equivalent direct evidence for any stronger public attribution. Do not request a new real-key signature.

Use neutral labels: launch signer, fee recipient, funder, observed controller, recipient. Distinguish cryptographic control, deployment, funding, economic benefit, public project affiliation and human identity. Shared funding, router/exchange use, timing, deterministic deployments, settlement destinations, and repeated counterparties alone do not establish common ownership, coordination, fraud or intent. Label wallet clustering as an inference with alternatives and exclusions.

Authenticate project statements from known control-linked channels and record dated provenance. Limit public attribution to what the evidence proves; do not search relatives, home addresses or unrelated personal accounts. Describe sale/rebuy routes as market-mediated redistribution unless additional evidence supports a stronger claim. Profit requires cost basis, fees, all material flows and retained inventory.

**Stop:** the needed public role/control relationship is established to the stated confidence, or additional investigation would become speculative/personal rather than resolve the token question.

**If incomplete:** preserve neutral role labels and state that common control, beneficiary, identity and intent remain unknown.
