# Activity, maturity and assessment

Preserve source-attributed public market activity, dated delivery, operating history
and their sampling limits. A mature token with meaningful public evidence should not
become mostly unknown simply because one source is unavailable. Look for a feasible
alternate within the original time/source budget, without retrying until favorable.

Top-account balances are not holder-population counts. Aggregate sampled spending
owners using the same-batch supply denominator, retaining custody exclusions and
beneficial-ownership uncertainty. Indexer volume/trade counts are broader source
claims; the verified sale receipts (at most ten) are only the verification sample. Record both
without inventing organic adoption, universal rankings or total trading history.

Current controls, liquidity principal/custody, holder economics and project maturity
answer different questions. A successful sale does not prove all intended sizes can
exit; a failed quote request does not establish a honeypot. High activity does not
neutralize freeze/seizure/mint/removal authority. The four conclusions should state
technical exposure, credibility/maturity, token economics and research confidence
separately, with material adverse facts and meaningful limits visible in each relevant
conclusion. Unknowns retain their actual reason and do not become passing checks.
