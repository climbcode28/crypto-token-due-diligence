# Decision review

Use four separate axes: technical exposure, credibility/maturity, token economics,
and research confidence. Each axis names the findings and/or coverage it discusses.
The verdict is `insufficient_evidence`, `conditional`, `favorable` or `adverse`.
All-gap evidence requires insufficient evidence. No number of favorable findings
is required, and missing access is neither a passing check nor an allegation.

Keep `coverage_gap` findings unresolved and Unverified. Adverse findings need usable
substantive evidence plus a stated basis, mechanism and consequence. State findings
and source publication claims are different; a project statement does not prove
runtime capability. Good market activity cannot neutralize a mint/freeze/withdrawal
power. Strength, confidence, impact and signal remain separate judgments.

High/critical adverse findings must appear in the summary, decision findings,
relevant axis and mitigation review. Mitigation statuses are unmitigated, partial
or mitigated; a mitigated claim needs usable evidence. Unresolved powers stay visible
even when the project has real delivery/adoption or an ordinary swap succeeded.

Completed decisions review the original request through exact quoted words,
findings and a met/not-met/unverified result. A probe amount never becomes a user
holding. Unknown requirements do not become met to finish the report. Preserve the
original question and focus unchanged.

Zero actions is valid. Actions can reflect a user choice or a response to an
identified risk; standard research still owed by the investigation cannot become
mandatory homework. The reviewer must assess interpretation, material contrary
evidence and whether the chosen conclusion is warranted; schema success alone
does not decide whether a token is safe or a suitable purchase.
